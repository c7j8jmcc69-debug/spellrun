Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email spellrun.app@icloud.com. We usually reply within two working days.

How does scoring work?

Each word scores chips × mult. Chips are the letter values added up (common letters are worth 1, Q and Z are worth 10). Mult is the word's length minus 2, so longer words multiply faster. Perks add chips or mult, or multiply your mult.

When does the daily run change?

At midnight UTC, which is the same moment for everyone. The app shows the time in your own time zone in Settings.

What counts toward my streak?

Finishing the daily run on its day, whether you clear all five rounds or not. Archive replays don't count.

Which words are allowed?

Spellrun accepts British and American spellings (colour and color, realise and realize): over 184,000 words of 3 letters or more, built from the public-domain ENABLE list and the British word lists in SCOWL. Proper nouns and abbreviations aren't included. Missing a word? Email us.

How do I restore the Full Game on a new iPhone?

Open Settings in Spellrun and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with. The Full Game also works for your Family Sharing group.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
