Spellrun

Spell words. Stack perks. Beat the daily.

Spellrun is a daily word game for iPhone with a roguelite twist. Every day, everyone gets the same letters. Spell words from your tiles to beat five score targets, and after each round pick one of three perks that bend the scoring rules. Share your result when you're done.

Free every day. The daily run is free, forever. Your streak, stats and history stay on your iPhone.

Full Game, one payment. Endless mode, the full archive of past dailies, and a +1 swap boost every run. No subscription.

Private by design. No account, no tracking, no data collection.
