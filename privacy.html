Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Spellrun ("the app") is made by Christian Augustuss Bergara ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

The short version: we don't collect any of your data. Your games, stats and settings stay on your iPhone.

What the app stores

The app stores your runs in progress, your results, stats and streaks, and your settings. All of it is kept only on your device. We have no servers and no accounts, and we can't see your data.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, sell or share personal data.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, or any third-party software development kits (SDKs).
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.

Sharing your result

When you tap Share result, the app opens the iOS share sheet with a short text summary of your run. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to share it.

Purchases

The optional Full Game is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment. We never receive your payment details, and we can't link your purchase to your identity. Apple's privacy policy applies to App Store purchases.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
